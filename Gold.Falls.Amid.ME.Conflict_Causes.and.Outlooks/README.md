# Gold Price Analysis 2026: Middle East Conflict Impact

This repository contains an analytical review and technical breakdown of the gold price (XAU/USD) collapse in March 2026. The project investigates the paradoxical behavior of an asset traditionally seen as a **"safe haven"** that nonetheless dropped 20-25% despite a major escalation in geopolitical tensions.

---

## 📌 Key Insights
* **Safe Haven Paradox:** Unlike the 1970s crises, surging oil prices (>$110) and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz did not trigger a gold rally.
* **The Fed Factor:** A "Hawkish" monetary policy (rates at 3.5–3.75%) kept real bond yields in positive territory, making them a more attractive alternative to non-yielding gold.
* **Dollar Dominance:** The strengthening USD, acting as the world’s primary liquidity source, created a strong inverse correlation that dragged gold prices down.
* **Liquidity Flush:** The rapid decline was accelerated by a wave of margin calls among institutional players and massive profit-taking following the 2025 rally.

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Tools

The following tools were utilized for data processing, analysis, and reporting:

### 1. **Python (Data Science & Visualization)**
* **Pandas:** Used for time-series processing, date parsing, and calculating technical indicators.
* **Matplotlib:** Employed to visualize price action and overlay **EMA 50/100/200** (Exponential Moving Averages).
* **Technical Analysis:** The script calculates EMAs to identify support zones and assess trend exhaustion.

### 2. **SQL (Data Management)**
* **Storage:** Historical price data (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume) was aggregated into a relational database.
* **Queries:** SQL was used to filter high-volatility periods (Feb–March 2026) and to calculate the correlation coefficient between Gold and the Dollar Index (DXY).

### 3. **Reporting & Table Construction**
* **Excel / Google Sheets:** Comparative data tables—such as inflation vs. Fed interest rate spreads—were built in spreadsheet software for rapid validation of macro data before final documentation.

## 🔍 Methodology & Conclusion Logic

The conclusion that the current drop represents a **correction** rather than a global trend reversal was based on three pillars:

1.  **Technical Indicators:** Prices remain above the critical **200-day EMA** support level, and the **RSI** reached "oversold" territory, which historically precedes a rebound.
2.  **Fundamental Strength:** Structural drivers remain intact, including soaring US national debt (>$39T), steady central bank accumulation, and rising mining costs reported by industry leaders like Barrick Gold.
3.  **Institutional Consensus:** Analysis of forecasts from major investment banks (**J.P. Morgan, Deutsche Bank**) shows they have maintained their $6,000+ year-end targets, viewing the volatility as temporary.
